# Flipkart-sales-dashboard

An interactive sales analytics dashboard designed to analyze Flipkart's sales performance, track key business metrics, and identify revenue trends across product categories and cities.

## 📌 1. Project Overview

The **Flipkart Sales Dashboard** provides a visual overview of sales performance for FY-2026. It transforms sales data into meaningful insights through KPI cards, charts, and product-level analysis.

The dashboard helps users understand revenue distribution, monitor order performance, identify top-selling products, and compare sales across different categories and cities.

### 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze overall sales and revenue performance.
- Identify top-performing products and product categories.
- Compare revenue across cities.
- Visualize relationships between product prices and revenue.
- Present key business metrics in a clear, interactive dashboard.

### 🔹 Revenue by Category
- Compares revenue across Clothing, Electronics, and Groceries.
- Helps identify high-performing product categories.
- Uses a bar chart for easy comparison.

### 🔹 Top-Selling Products
- Displays product IDs and product names.
- Highlights products contributing to sales performance.
- Makes it easier to identify important products.

### 🔹 Revenue by City
- Compares revenue across cities, including Chennai, Kolkata, Mumbai, Delhi, and Bangalore.
- Helps identify cities generating higher revenue.
- Uses a horizontal bar chart for comparison.

### 🔹 Category-Wise Revenue Distribution
- Displays the contribution of Clothing, Electronics, and Groceries to total revenue.
- Uses a doughnut chart to show category-wise revenue proportions.

### 🔹 Price vs Revenue Analysis
- Visualizes revenue values across product records.
- Helps explore revenue variations and identify significant spikes.
- Uses a line chart to display changes across the dataset.
